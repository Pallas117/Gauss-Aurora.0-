Private mesh transport and provenance boundaries.
## Receive
Read-only connectivity and authorization evidence.
## Produce
Bounded transport plans and verification reports.
## Guardrails
No unapproved network mutation, spacecraft command, or secret disclosure.
## Interoperability
Use shared truth states and transport adapter contracts.
## Subagent lanes
mesh-observer, mesh-builder, mesh-verifier, mesh-operator.
## Required workflow
Observe, diagnose, authorize, execute only approved bounded changes, verify.
