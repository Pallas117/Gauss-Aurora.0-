# Satellite operations role

## Own
Read-only satellite operations analysis and operator-facing evidence.
## Receive
Freshness, provenance, and mission-boundary inputs.
## Produce
Advisories, screening results, and approval-gated handoffs.
## Guardrails
No automatic spacecraft commands or qualification claims from screening data. Any command-readiness handoff requires explicit human authorization before uplink or execution.
## Interoperability
Use the seven-zone and operator-task contracts.
## Subagent lanes
operations-observer, operations-analyst, handoff-operator.
## Required workflow
Observe, classify truth state, screen, verify, and hand off.
