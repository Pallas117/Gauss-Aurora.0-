# Project Gauss — Vision, Roadmap & Classifications

**Classification:** INTERNAL — Review before external share.

## Vision

Project Gauss aims to be the **ultimate operations problem-solving tool** for space situational awareness and beyond, built on frontier technology:

- **Agentic AI** — Implemented in the **orchestrator** and in **AI research and data analysis models**; we are evolving them into a strong **R&D tool** for future inventions and productisation.
- **Post-quantum secured cybersecurity** — Sovereign, zero-trust security ready for a post-quantum threat model (NIST/ISO-aligned, minimal attack surface).

This foundation supports a value chain from **today’s SSA and space weather** through **in-orbit servicing and manufacturing** to **asteroid mining**:

1. **SSA & operations today** — Live space weather, threat fusion, pass orchestration, operator cockpit.
2. **Value from the space environment** — Value is captured on a **blockchain-backed protocol**, designed to be **comprehensive, equitable, and future-proof** for the long-term **intergalactic economy**. The framework will be **legally ratified** before rollout and advanced through trusted channels (e.g. **Secure World Foundation**, **Space Debris DAO**, **RAND Corporation** contacts). Models analyze **materials** (e.g. debris composition, salvageable assets) and **data** (telemetry, conjunction, environment); value is **tokenised** and **captured** for use in orbit.
3. **In-orbit servicing & manufacturing** — Tokenised value and analytics feed servicing missions, on-orbit manufacturing, and resource logistics.
4. **Asteroid mining** — Same tooling and value framework extended to NEA and main-belt targets.

**Ceres & Pallas.** Gauss, as a scientist, mapped the orbits of the dwarf planets **Ceres** and **Pallas**. In that spirit, a long-term goal of Project Gauss is to support and ultimately **launch missions to Ceres and Pallas** — from mapping orbits to enabling resource missions. Both appear in the public narrative.

**Near-term focus.** Space debris and conflict are the primary infrastructural and humanitarian challenges we are designed to help address.

## Operator progression

**Target operator order:** MEO/GEO satellite operators → **ISAM** (in-space servicing, assembly, manufacturing) → Space Station operators → deep space operators.

Until the system is learned, capable, and trusted, we remain **horizontally receptive** to problems heard in the industry and leverage existing contacts.

---

## Public platform

The **public face** is an open, **fun, mysterious, and impactful** platform (apps, quests, missions) that educates and prepares the next generation to join. The architecture **prepares for** full security clearances and very sensitive datasets from defence and space agencies, even if current deployment is not yet under strict classification.

---

## Hardware and in-space validation

The **8GB M1 constraint** is a deliberate **control**: it proves the stack runs on limited, space-like hardware so that **parts of the software can be validated for deployment on hardware in space**, speeding up in-space validation and future in-space datacenter integration.

## Compliance and geopolitics

We are building for **NIST, ISO 27001, ECSS**, and related audits, and aim to be **geopolitically agnostic** to support dialogue and peace. Export and control regimes (e.g. ITAR) are **to be determined**; design will allow for future compliance where required.

## Privacy & Secrecy Classifications

### Purpose

Classifications define how information and data are handled, who may access them, and what may be shared with operators, partners, or the wider world.

### Classification levels

| Level | Label | Description | Typical use |
|-------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **PUBLIC** | Unclassified / Public | Safe for public release, marketing, open documentation. No PII, no mission-critical or partner-restricted detail. | README, public docs, blog posts, conference talks. |
| **INTERNAL** | Internal / Operator | For authorised operators and internal teams. May include system design, API contracts, runbooks. Not for partner or public distribution without approval. | Architecture docs, operator runbooks, internal roadmap. |
| **RESTRICTED** | Restricted / Confidential | Mission-specific, partner, or commercially sensitive. Access on need-to-know; handling per agreement or policy. | Partner data, mission telemetry, tokenisation schema, servicing/manufacturing plans. |
| **CONTROLLED** | Controlled / Export-sensitive | Subject to export control or sovereign handling. Access and transfer restricted by policy and law. | Crypto implementations, post-quantum modules, certain AI/ML models. |

### Need-to-know and obfuscation

Sensitive identifiers, roadmaps, and mission targets are kept **need-to-know** or **obfuscated** until the right trust sequences and actions are completed. Long-term narratives are built in collaboration to quickly unblock infrastructural challenges (e.g. space debris, conflict).

### Data and asset classification

| Data / asset type | Default classification | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|--------|
| Public documentation | PUBLIC | No secrets, no PII. |
| Architecture, API contracts, operator guides | INTERNAL | Restrict distribution outside org/operators. |
| User/operator PII | RESTRICTED | Minimise collection; retain per privacy policy. |
| Mission telemetry, pass plans, threat assessments | RESTRICTED or INTERNAL | By mission/customer agreement. |
| Tokenised value schemas, servicing/manufacturing specs | RESTRICTED | Until approved for partner or public. |
| Cryptographic and post-quantum implementation details | CONTROLLED | Handle per export and security policy. |
| Ceres/Pallas mission and mining roadmap detail | INTERNAL or RESTRICTED | By stakeholder and partner agreement. |
